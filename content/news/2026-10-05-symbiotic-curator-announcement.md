Presto is providing instant liquidity for tokenized assets on Liquid Lane. Redemption requests route to liquidity providers through an on-chain RFQ, Presto fills them just-in-time, and holders get stablecoins instantly while issuer settlement completes in the background. A single capital base captures redemption spreads, deploys on demand, and earns lending yield in external markets between redemptions. Behind the fills is a decade-long algorithmic trading franchise, built and run in-house.
