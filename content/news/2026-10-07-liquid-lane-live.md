As of October 6, Presto is live on Symbiotic's Liquid Lane, filling redemptions for tokenized assets just-in-time. A holder requests an exit through an on-chain RFQ, Presto fills it, and they hold USDC seconds later while issuer settlement completes in the background. One capital base captures redemption spreads, deploys on demand, and earns lending yield on Morpho between fills, so instant exits do not cost idle capital.
