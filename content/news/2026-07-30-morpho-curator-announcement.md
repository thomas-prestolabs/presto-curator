Two USDC vaults are live on Ethereum. Prime lends against blue-chip assets, and Forte lends against selected RWA collateral, including 3F assets (wFalconX, wJAAA) through Presto's partnership with 3F. Every collateral asset is vetted before the vaults lend against it: oracle configurations are verified against onchain state, Presto participates directly in liquidations, and supply is managed across markets to keep rates where they should be.
